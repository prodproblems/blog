## About Real Production Problems

Real Production Problems is an engineering learning project focused on practical production failures and the decisions engineers make under pressure.

The goal is to develop strong incident judgment—not just explain a technology. Each breakdown distinguishes observed symptoms, hypotheses, the confirmed or representative failure mechanism, immediate mitigation, permanent repair, operational trade-offs, and prevention.

Unless a specific public incident and its sources are cited, scenarios are explicitly labelled as representative examples. Example metrics are illustrative.

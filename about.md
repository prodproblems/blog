<div class="simple-page">
  <p class="eyebrow">ABOUT THE PROJECT</p>
  <h1>Learn from production failures.</h1>
  <p class="lead">Real Production Problems explains the engineering decisions behind real-world failure patterns—clearly, practically, and without unnecessary noise.</p>

  <h2>What this site is for</h2>
  <p>Knowing a technology is not the same as knowing what to do when production starts failing. Each breakdown focuses on the signals, investigation, customer impact, mitigation, root cause, trade-offs, and prevention.</p>

  <h2>Our approach</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Observe:</strong> separate symptoms from assumptions.</li>
    <li><strong>Diagnose:</strong> test hypotheses with evidence.</li>
    <li><strong>Recover:</strong> reduce customer impact safely.</li>
    <li><strong>Prevent:</strong> improve tests, alerts, limits, and runbooks.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Accuracy matters</h2>
  <p>We distinguish documented public incidents from representative scenarios. Illustrative examples and metrics are labelled clearly; invented postmortems are not presented as facts.</p>

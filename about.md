  <h1>Production is where <span>the real lessons</span> begin.</h1>
  <p class="about-lede">Real Production Problems turns difficult backend incidents into practical engineering judgment—one failure, one investigation, and one better decision at a time.</p>
  <div class="about-rule"><span>OUR MISSION</span><span>01 / WHY WE EXIST</span></div>
  <section class="about-story">
    <div><p class="about-label">NOT ANOTHER TUTORIAL</p><h2>Learn to think through the failure.</h2></div>
    <div><p>Knowing a technology is not the same as knowing what to do when a production system starts failing. The hard part is connecting symptoms to evidence, protecting customers, choosing a safe mitigation, and proving the root cause.</p><p>That is what this project is built to teach: the decisions behind reliable systems, explained with practical examples and real engineering trade-offs.</p></div>
  </section>
  <div class="about-rule"><span>THE METHOD</span><span>02 / EVERY BREAKDOWN</span></div>
  <div class="about-method">
    <article><span>01</span><h3>Observe</h3><p>Start with what the system and customers are actually telling us.</p></article>
    <article><span>02</span><h3>Diagnose</h3><p>Test competing hypotheses using metrics, logs, traces, and query plans.</p></article>
    <article><span>03</span><h3>Recover</h3><p>Reduce the blast radius before attempting a risky permanent fix.</p></article>
    <article><span>04</span><h3>Prevent</h3><p>Turn the incident into better tests, alerts, limits, and runbooks.</p></article>
  </div>
